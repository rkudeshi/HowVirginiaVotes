Last Content Update: Last week

General Election on Tuesday, November 3, 2026

The General Election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, with ballots including contests for the offices of U.S. Senator (6-year term) and U.S. Representative for the 8th District (2-year term). There will also be a Special Election with three (3) amendments to the Virginia Constitution for consideration.

Sample ballot for the Commonwealth of Virginia, City of Falls Church General & Special Elections on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (First Ward - 001), showing candidate races for U.S. Senate and House 8th District, plus Constitutional Amendment Questions 1 and 2.

Sample Ballot - Page 2

Page 2 of the Commonwealth of Virginia, City of Falls Church official sample ballot for November 3, 2026 (First Ward - 001), containing Proposed Constitutional Amendment Question 3 regarding voting rights.

Important Voting & Election Update: By-Mail & Early Voting

By-Mail Ballots Are On The Way

By-mail ballots are currently being processed and mailed out. If you requested a mail-in ballot, it should arrive in your mailbox soon. Please wait for delivery before contacting our office. If you have requested a ballot but have not received it by Thursday, October 1, 2026, please contact the Voter Registration and Elections Office.

In-Person Early Voting

In-person early voting begins Friday, September 18, 2026, at 8:00 a.m.

Return Your Ballot via Official Drop Box

Voters concerned about USPS postal delivery times are strongly encouraged to use the City's secure Official Ballot Drop Box to return their completed mail-in ballot.

General Election Timeline

  • Friday, September 18, 2026: Early Voting for the General Election begins, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Thursday, October 1, 2026: Voters who have applied for a permanent by-mail ballot or a ballot for just this election should receive that ballot no later than this date. Please contact our office if you have not received your ballot by then.
  • Monday, October 12, 2026: Closed for the Holiday
  • Sunday, October 18, 2026: First Sunday of Early Voting 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.
  • Friday, October 23, 2026: Last day to register to vote in person by 5 p.m., online by 11:59 p.m., by-mail (PDF) must be postmarked October 23.
  • Friday, October 23, 2026: 5 p.m. is the deadline to apply for an absentee by-mail (PDF) ballot.
  • Saturday, October 24, 2026: First Saturday, Early Voting 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Sunday, October 25, 2026: Last Sunday of Early Voting 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.
  • Thursday, October 29, 2026: Early Voting stays open late until 7 p.m.
  • Saturday, October 31, 2026: Last Day of Early Voting, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Sunday, November 1, 2026: Closed
  • Monday, November 2, 2026:Emergency voting only (PDF), 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.
  • Tuesday, November 3, 2026, Election Day: Polls are open at your assigned polling place 6 a.m. to 7 p.m.

  1. Quick Links for Candidates
  • If you want to be a political party's nominee, please contact the chairperson of the local party to begin your filing process.
  • If you want to run for any office as an independent, especially City Council or School Board, please read below.
    • Candidate Forms
    • Summary of Laws and Policies Candidate Campaign Committees
    • Dos and Don’ts for Campaigners and Authorized Representatives (SBE 604 guidance) (PDF)
    • Guidelines for Campaigners and Authorized Representatives (PDF)
  • There are two separate but related parts:
    • Campaign Finance is the accounting of raising and spending funds, materials, and services in order to advocate that people vote for you.
    • Ballot Access means getting your name on the ballot.
      Each of these parts have their own date schedules and deadlines.
  • Candidates may start to gather petitions (PDF) (125 valid, city registered signatures for City Council and School Board)
  • Candidates may start to file ballot access documents with our office. Please note: Before we can accept the Petition of Qualified Voters, candidates must submit the following 2 documents
    • Certificate of Candidate Qualification - Local Offices (PDF)
    • Commonwealth of Virginia Declaration of Candidacy (PDF)
  • The Deadline for City Council and School Board and all Independent Candidates to file is 7 p.m. on Tuesday, June 17, the date of the Primary Election(s). 
  • For more information, visit the Virginia Department of Elections.
