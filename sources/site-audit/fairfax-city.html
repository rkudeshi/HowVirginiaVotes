Elections Resources

Register to Vote

Learn how to register to vote in Fairfax City. Discover online, mail-in, and in-person options, key deadlines, eligibility requirements, and steps to update or cancel your registration.

Absentee Voting

Explore absentee voting options in Fairfax City, including vote-by-mail and early voting. Apply online or by mail, return ballots via post or drop box, and track your status. Early voting typically starts 46 days before election day.

How To Vote

Find essential information about Election Day voting, including polling locations, procedures, and the equipment used in the City of Fairfax. Explore resources to help ensure a smooth and efficient voting process.

Officers of Election

Serve as an Officer of Election in Fairfax City. Assist with voter check-ins, manage polling equipment, and ensure elections run smoothly. Training is mandatory. Apply online to get started.

Sample Ballots

View sample ballots for upcoming elections in the City of Fairfax.

Election Calendar

View the current election calendar and schedule for the City of Fairfax.

Run for Office

Interested in running for office? Learn about eligibility requirements, candidate filing deadlines, campaign finance, and political signage guidelines for the City of Fairfax elections.

Public Notices

Stay updated on public notices, including official orders, meeting agendas, and reschedules for the Electoral Board. Find information about upcoming meetings and past notices for the City of Fairfax elections.

Election Results

View official results for upcoming and past elections in the City of Fairfax, including general, primary, and special elections. Access results for the 2025 elections and more.

Electoral Board

Electoral Board members work to ensure that elections are conducted in a fair and transparent manner. View the Electoral Board members, meeting minutes, and policies here.
