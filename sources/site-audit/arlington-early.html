Early Voting

Early Voting Locations & Hours

November 3, 2026 General & Special Election Information

  • Voters need an acceptable form of ID.
  • Voters mark and cast their ballot on the spot. They only need to bring an acceptable ID. 
  • Curbside voting is available at all Early Voting locations during operating hours for voters with disabilities or those aged 65 and older. Learn more about Accessibility Services.

There is no in-person voting at the Madison Community Center, as the facility is closed for a maintenance project.
