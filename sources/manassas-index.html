September 08, 2025

Dean Drive Closures

Crews are replacing the water main on Dean Drive between the city’s water storage tanks site and the SERVE Family Shelter. Dean Drive will be closed to through traffic with a detour in place. Local traffic should still have access from at least one side of Dean Drive.

As the work continues, water service on Dean Drive may be interrupted to make connections to the new service line, but crews will limit disruption as much as possible.
The work is part of a Capital Improvement Project to upgrade the existing 24 inch transmission main with a new 36 inch water main and improve the city’s water service for the future. If you have any questions, call the Water & Sewer Department at 703-257-8380. Thank you for your patience.

Immigration in Manassas City  //  Inmigración en la Ciudad de Manassas

(Hazle clic en el título del artículo para leer en español)
Immigration Enforcement Community Concerns
The City of Manassas has received questions recently regarding Immigration Enforcement by the Manassas City Police Department (MCPD), including false information that they will be conducting immigration checkpoints and/or “raids”. The Manassas City Police Department has not, and will not undertake any type of immigration related investigations. We are not authorized to enforce federal immigration laws and cannot lawfully do so. If any members of our community, regardless of immigration status, have questions or concerns about the legitimacy of an event or officer, they are encouraged to call 703-257-8000 in order to receive accurate and factual information regarding this issue.
