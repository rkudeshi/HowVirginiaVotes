Voter Registration

The Voter Registration Office maintains voter rolls, processes potential candidates and handles campaign finance reports.  The staff is knowledgeable in federal, state, and local laws/codes. The office and its three-member Electoral Board is responsible for virtually all aspects of voter registration and election administration within the city of Manassas Park.


Work the Polls


Help support our democracy by joining our team of officers that help preserve the integrity of our elections.

The Manassas Park Electoral Board will conduct a Provisional & Canvass Meeting for the November General on the following upcoming dates and times:

11/04/2026 at 11am. - (Provisional & Canvass Meeting)
11/06/2026 at 3pm. - (Canvass Reconvenes)
11/09/2026 at TIME TBD. - (Provisional Meeting & Certification of Election)

The meetings will take place at the Voter Registration office at 100 Park Central Plaza, Suite 103. The public is invited to attend. If you have any questions, please contact the Voter Registration office at (703) 335-8806 or via email: p.brendel@manassasparkva.gov.

To vote absentee (by mail), please visit here to apply for one to be mailed to you.

To vote absentee (in person), there will be a 45 day period prior to November 3rd where you can visit the General Registrar at City Hall to vote in person early. You DO NOT need to apply in order to vote absentee in person. 

City Hall will be open for In-Person Early Voting starting on Friday, September 18th, 2026, from 8:30AM to 4:30PM. Drop boxes will also be available for you at the City Hall General Registrar's office to drop off your mailed absentee ballots beginning on September 18th through November 2nd. Election Day (November 3rd, 2026), you may find your drop boxes at your assigned precincts.

For the 45 Days of Early Voting, our office hours are:

  • September 18th to October 30th
    • Monday – Friday, 8:30AM to 4:30PM

We will have 2 Early Voting Saturdays available closer to Election Day (November 3rd):

  • October 24th from 9:00AM to 5:00PM 
  • October 31st from 9:00AM to 5:00PM

We will have 2 Early Voting Sundays available closer to Election Day (November 3rd):

  • October 18th from 11:00AM to 4:00PM 
  • October 25th from 11:00AM to 4:00PM

 

Deadlines

  • October 23rd   - Last Day to register to vote by:
    • 5:00PM for mail/in person
    • 11:59PM for online
  • October 23rd - Last Day to apply to vote by mail applications by 5:00PM
  • October 31st – Last Day to vote early, in person by 5:00PM
    • There will not be any Early Voting on November 2nd.

Find Your Polling Location


Manassas Park has three polling locations labelled as follows:

Precinct One: Manassas Park High School Gymnasium, 8200 Euclid Avenue

Precinct Two: Manassas Park Community Center, 99 Adams Street

Precinct Three: Manassas Park City Hall, 100 Park Central Plaza, Suite 103
