Early Voting Office Locations

Early voting for the Nov. 3 election begins Sept. 18. The last day to vote early in-person is Oct. 31.

All localities offer early voting. Voters are only allowed to vote early in the locality in which they are registered.

Each locality in Virginia sets its own early voting location(s). Some localities offer early voting only at the general registrar's office, while other localities have early voting in locations other than the registrar’s office. To find all early voting locations and ballot dropbox information for your locality, contact your general registrar's office.
