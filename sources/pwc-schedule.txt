Dates Locations GENERAL I NFORMATION For the 45 days prior to all elections, PWC voters can vote at any Prince William County early voting location during open hours. Early voting is generally open dur ing the week and is also open on the two Saturdays immediately before Election Day.  The last day of early voting is the Saturday prior to Election Day.   The Office of Elections at 9250 Lee Avenue in Manassas, VA is always an early voting location starting 45 days prior to the election.  Satellite locations for early voting vary both in number, location, and hours for each election specified by local ordinance.     Early voting does require that a voter shows an acceptable form of ID . ​ Curbside Voting: Available for seniors and voters with disabilities at all in-person voting locations in designated parking spaces. Drive-thru voting is not available. ​ HELPFUL LINKS​ ​Voters ​ Acceptable IDs List Polling Place List Election District Map ​ ​ ​ ​ Citizens PWC Elected Officials Political Party Information Inactive Cancelled List (2025) ​ ​ Candidates Becoming A Candidate PWC Candidate Interest Form ​ State Election Results Register Online Voter Forms Prince William County Office of Elections Office Hours: 8:30am to 4:30pm (M-F) 9250 Lee Ave Suite 1, Manassas, VA 20110 Phone: 703-792-6470 Fax: 703-792-6461 Email: pwcvotes@pwcgov.org Follow us County Homepage © 2026 PWC Office of Elections bottom of page
