Announcements    Upcoming Electoral Board (EB) Meetings:    The Manassas Park Electoral Board will conduct a Provisional & Canvass Meeting for the November General on the following upcoming dates and times: 11/04/2026 at 11am. - (Provisional & Canvass Meeting) 11/06/2026 at 3pm. - (Canvass Reconvenes) 11/09/2026 at TIME TBD . - (Provisional Meeting & Certification of Election) The meetings will take place at the Voter Registration office at 100 Park Central Plaza, Suite 103. The public is invited to attend. If you have any questions, please contact the Voter Registration office at (703) 335-8806 or via email: p.brendel@manassasparkva.gov .    November General / Special Election      Manassas Park is having a November General Election/ Special Election on November 03 rd , 2026. This election will include races for: United States Senate House of Representatives 10th District House of Delegates District 20 Special Election Manassas Park City Council Member (3 seats)  Proposed Constitutional Amendment #1 Proposed Constitutional Amendment #2 Proposed Constitutional Amendment #3              (Click Here For Sample Ballot)   See more information on the Virginia Department of Elections' webpage on upcoming elections :  Upcoming Elections Link:  Click Here Candidates and Referendums Link:  Click Here For information regarding recent Felony Voting Rights changes, click below: Click Here To vote absentee (by mail) , please visit  here  to apply for one to be mailed to you. To vote absentee (in person) , there will be a 45 day period prior to November 3 rd where you can visit the General Registrar at City Hall to vote in person early.   You DO NOT need to apply in order to vote absentee in person.  City Hall will be open for In-Person Early Voting starting on Friday, September 18th, 2026, from 8:30AM to 4:30PM. Drop boxes will also be available for you at the City Hall General Registrar's office to drop off your mailed absentee ballots beginning on September 18 th through November 2 nd . Election Day (November 3 rd , 2026), you may find your drop boxes at your assigned precincts. For the 45 Days of Early Voting, our office hours are: September 18 th  to October 30 th Monday – Friday, 8:30AM to 4:30PM We will have 2 Early Voting Saturdays available closer to Election Day (November 3 rd ): October 24 th from 9:00AM to 5:00PM  October 31 st  from 9:00AM to 5:00PM We will have 2 Early Voting Sundays available closer to Election Day (November 3 rd ): October 18 th from 11:00AM to 4:00PM  October 25 th  from 11:00AM to 4:00PM   Deadlines October 23 rd     - Last Day to register to vote by: 5:00PM for mail/in person 11:59PM for online October 23 rd  - Last Day to apply to vote by mail applications by 5:00PM October 31 st  – Last Day to vote early, in person by 5:00PM There will not be any Early Voting on November 2 nd .
